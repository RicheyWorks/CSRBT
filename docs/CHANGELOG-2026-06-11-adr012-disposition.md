# CHANGELOG 2026-06-11 — ADR-012 disposition: Accepted; instruments were the deliverable

The reconciliation move, in the ADR-009/ADR-010 tradition: ADR-012 was Proposed with a
staged E1–E6 build, and one day later its instrument phase (E1, E2, E3, E3b, E3c) was
complete: three negative verdicts and two findings sharper than their theses, all
published with receipts. Leaving the ADR "Proposed" forever would
misstate the record; pretending E4–E6 were still simply queued would misstate it worse
— two of the three lost their premises *to measurement*. Docs-only slice; suite **529
green** (unchanged).

## What changed

- **ADR-012 → Accepted (disposition 2026-06-11), new §8.** The instrument phase is the
  deliverable: the performance artifact (a calibrated selector whose "match best fixed
  without hindsight, don't chase blocks" was proven the structural optimum by E3c) and
  the evolutionary artifact (viability sliver, collapse attribution flipped to the
  filter, exploration priced at the seam).
- **E4 parked, premise measured away twice:** no re-adaptation lag worth cutting (E3),
  and no switching architecture that can make chasing regimes pay at this granularity
  (E3c). A diversity mechanism cannot cut a lag that does not exist with a quantum
  that costs more than the prize.
- **E5 parked by its own gate** (E4 working — the ADR's words: otherwise it is Option
  B and repeats V5).
- **E6 untouched:** machinery transfer is orthogonal to every negative on the record;
  stays staged, optional, long.
- **Re-arming triggers named in §8, falsifiable, dormant:** (1) regime blocks long
  enough that E3c's arithmetic flips claimable — re-arms E4 and the retired recency
  item; (2) a switching mechanism cheaper than both measured quanta — re-arms E4 and
  re-prices E3c; (3) E4 landing re-arms E5.
- README design-history entry updated to match.

## Why Accepted and not Superseded/Rejected

The ADR's load-bearing decision was the *reframe* (§2: two first-class outputs, "more
measurably alive, not just a tuned number") and the *method* (instruments before
mechanisms, falsifiable theses, negatives published). Both were executed in full and
both held up — the method is precisely what caught E4's premise before E4 was built.
The parked mechanisms are not failures of the ADR; they are its method working as
specified.
