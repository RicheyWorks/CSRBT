# ADR-127 — The board: every ledger, rendered, never typed

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Gives the harness one page that says what it can vouch for, generated from its ledgers, with a check that fails when the page and the ledgers disagree**

## 1. Seven ledgers and no reader

The harness keeps its claims in ledgers: `counts.json` (every suite's
count), `walk_ledger.json` (the robot on every target, both transports,
every page), `task_ledger.json` (runs and traces), `ecosystem_ledger.json`
(the engines' own suites), `routes.json` (the pages) — and, until today,
the mutant runners' verdicts in **no ledger at all**: six runners printed a
line ("31 killed, 0 survived, 4 equivalent") that the next ADR copied by
hand. The only place all of it was read together was an ADR's closing
paragraph, typed on the day and stale the next. The Atlas taught the rule
in ADR-120: a published page carries no number a tool did not write, and a
check fails when the page and its sources disagree.

## 2. The decision

**`tools/mutant_ledger.py`** — every mutant runner (`mutate_organism`,
`mutate_lab`, `mutate_mcp`, `mutate_walk`, `mutate_tasks`,
`mutate_harness`) records its run: mutants, killed, survived,
inconclusive, recorded equivalents, and a row per mutant with its verdict,
merged per runner into `tools/mutant_ledger.json`.

**`tools/harness_board.py`** renders `tools/harness_board.html` whole from
the seven ledgers: a verdict line, five headline tiles, the ten harness
suites, the robot's six target walks with the snapshot's price, every
routed page with its driven/refused/unreachable counts, every task with
its run and its trace and the trace's economy, every runner, every
engine's suite against its floor. Nothing on it is edited by hand; the
prose that frames a section lives in the script. `--check` says whether
the file is what the ledgers render. It is published as an artifact (the
Harness Board), beside the Atlas, and republished with each render.

**`tools/verify/verify_board.py`** (**37 checks**) holds the file to the
ledgers and the ledgers to each other: the committed board is
byte-for-byte the render; the summary's arithmetic is the ledgers' sums;
every `mutate_*.py` in `tools/` is on the board and in the ledger, and its
ledger row is its own catalogue (as many mutants as it lists, as many
equivalents as it records, killed + survived + inconclusive = mutants, the
totals agreeing with the rows); every walked page and every task is on the
page; no `None` or `NaN` leaks into the text; both themes; determinism.

## 3. First render

    4882 / 4882 suite checks · 66 suites, 66 green, no holes
    6893 commands walked · 3 targets × 2 transports, 41 pages
    8 / 8 tasks held · 6 / 6 traces held · 134 expectations confirmed
    104 / 104 mutants killed · 0 survived · 0 inconclusive · 8 equivalent
    1629 engine tests · 15 suites · 0 failures

The 4882 is the suites' own sum; `run_all`'s 5251 adds the audits, which
have no counts row. The board says so beside the tile.

## 4. Held

- The board is not a kit page: it lives under `tools/` beside the ledgers
  it renders, not under `docs/`, so it is neither routed nor walked nor
  audited as a page. The kit's page rules are for the pages the kit
  ships to readers; the board is an instrument's readout, published.
- It is republished by hand after a render. A render that is not
  republished is a board that is behind; `verify_board` holds the file,
  not the artifact.
