Ethogram — tap to score behavior

Big buttons for the field: tap a behavior each time you see it (focal) or at each scan. The timer flashes every scan interval. Undo takes back the last tap. Hold a card to rename it.

Quadrats — count per frame

One box per quadrat: tap + for every individual inside the frame. Dispersion updates live — random, regular, or clumped.

Species counts — the site survey

Photographs

A tally says how many. A photograph says what they were — and it is the only part of a day's counting that another person can check.

The export carries the reference, not the image. The frames stay in this tab and are never uploaded; closing it discards them, and the originals are in your camera roll. What goes into the .eco lines and the CSV is the filename, a CRC-32 and when the file was taken — enough to put the row and the frame back together later. The CRC is an integrity check, not a cryptographic one.
