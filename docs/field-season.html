Field Season
part of the ecology kit →

One season. One meadow. Twelve days.

A hidden meadow is generated from the season number — its species list, abundances, spatial pattern, and the vole population are all unknown to you. Spend your twelve field days sampling however you like, then file your report. It gets peer-reviewed against the truth. Same season number = same meadow, so a class can compare strategies fairly.

A teaching game: the meadow is a simplified world, but every number you get is sampled with the same statistics the real instruments read — clumped quadrats really are overdispersed, recaptures really are rarer when the population is big.
