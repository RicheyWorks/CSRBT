Ecology Field Card

Every metric, what it tells you, its interpretation band, and the Workbench tool that computes it — one card to keep on the bench. Print it double-sided.

The mapping: keys are species, how often a key is touched is that species' abundance, inserts and removes are births and deaths, and time is counted in operations — so every number reproduces exactly. Your own entered field data reads the same way.
