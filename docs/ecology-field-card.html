CSRBT · quick reference

Ecology Field Card

Every metric the kit computes — what it tells you, its interpretation band, and the tool that produces it — across the Workbench, the forest and vegetation plots, the fungal survey, behavioural sampling, selection on marked individuals, and the cell and microbiology bench. One card to keep on the bench. Print it double-sided.

The mapping: keys are species, how often a key is touched is that species' abundance, inserts and removes are births and deaths, and time is counted in operations — so every number reproduces exactly. Your own entered field data reads the same way.

Vegetation plots

tool: Relevé — cover by stratum
MetricReads
Cover classordinal — convert to a class midpoint before averaging, and never average the class numbers
Shannon on coversame formula, but pi is cover share, not count share — say which you used
Mean Caverage coefficient of conservatism — <3.5 disturbed · >4.5 remnant-quality
FQImeanC × √N — rewards quality and richness; comparable only within one region's C-list
Adjusted FQI100(meanC/10)(√Nnat/√Ntot) — 0–100, penalises non-native load
Prevalence indexΣ(cover×weight)/Σcover — ≤3.0 passes the hydrophyte criterion. One of three, not a delineation
LPI coverhits/points, SE = √(p(1−p)/n) — plan the transect before walking it or n is meaningless

Behaviour

tool: Ethogram — sampling & time budgets
MetricReads
Time budgettime in state ÷ observed time — out-of-sight excluded, or every % is wrong
Point sample %points in state ÷ points; unbiased for time, useless for frequency. SE = √(p(1−p)/n)
One-zero %intervals containing the behaviour. Biased for duration and frequency both — never a budget or a rate
Event ratecount ÷ observed minutes. Point sampling cannot produce one
Mean boutcontinuous recording only; entirely a function of how the state's end was defined
Cohen's κ(po−pe)/(1−pe). Bands are convention, not result. Depressed when one category dominates
Transitionsfirst-order Markov; diagonal structurally empty. Rows under 5 transitions are noise

Selection & inheritance

tool: Selection Log — marked individuals
MetricReads
Repeatability RICC. <0.7 the trait as measured is mostly your hand. Attenuates β by ≈R — fix the method, not the n
Differential Sweighted mean − mean, in trait units. Includes selection on every correlated trait you didn't measure
Intensity iS/σ — comparable across traits and years
Gradient βslope of relative fitness on standardised trait. Univariate β = i exactly; only a multiple regression separates direct from indirect
h²mid-parent slope (single parent × 2). Upper bound — shared environment inflates it. Population- and environment-specific
R = h²Spredicted response. Routinely fails in the wild — a hypothesis the next generation tests

Cell bench

tool: Cell Bench — counting & assays
MetricReads
Counting CV1/√N. 100 cells = ±10%, 400 = ±5%. The floor on every count
cells/mL(cells ÷ squares) × chamber factor × dilution. 20–50 per square: fewer imprecise, more undercount
Viabilitytrypan blue is membrane integrity, not viability. <80% suspect handling first
Doubling timeΔt ÷ log₂(N_f/N_i). Exponential phase only; >80% confluent makes it too long
Standard curvehigh r² ≠ valid. Above the top standard is extrapolation, not measurement — dilute
A260/A280≈1.8 DNA, ≈2.0 RNA — conventions, buffer-sensitive, silent about integrity
Mitotic indexdividing ÷ total. Phase durations assume asynchrony, known cycle, equal visibility — the last is false

Micro bench

tool: Micro Bench — plates & growth
MetricReads
CFU/mLcolonies ÷ (volume × dilution). A clump or chain is one CFU — never cells/mL
30–300 rule<30 TFTC, imprecise · >300 TNTC, a bias more plates can't fix. Show what you excluded
Adjacent platestwo countable dilutions should agree within ≈2×. Wider means the series, not the sample
µ (h⁻¹)slope of ln(OD) over exponential phase; t_d = ln2/µ. You choose the window, not the software
OD₆₀₀turbidity, not cells. Saturates optically above ≈0.4–0.6. No OD→CFU without your own calibration
Zone (mm)a measurement. S/I/R needs a breakpoint table, revised annually — enter yours and record its edition
Negative controlgrows? the run is void, not adjusted
