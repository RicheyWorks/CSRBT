Before you trust a number

What this suite will and will not tell you

Compost is unusually well served by published standards, so most of this bench sits in the first bucket. The exceptions are named.

ships · regulation PFRP time and temperature 40 CFR 503 Appendix B: 55 °C for 3 days in-vessel or static aerated pile; 55 °C for 15 days with 5 turnings for a windrow. Cited, not paraphrased.
ships · standard USDA NOP compost requirements Initial C:N of 25:1 to 40:1, and the same temperature-and-turning requirements, stated as the NOP states them.
ships · arithmetic C:N of a blend Total carbon over total nitrogen on dry mass — a calculation, checkable by hand, with the averaging mistake named beside it.
ships · standard Texture by feel The USDA ribbon-and-grit key, which is a defined procedure with a defined outcome.
labelled a convention Mix indices Drainage, water-holding and aeration are ordinal ranks on this page, and it says so. They are not measurements and must not be averaged.
editable, not asserted Feedstock C:N values Published values for the same material differ by a factor of two, so every ingredient's value is editable and the default is a starting point.
Where a standard ends and judgement begins

What the standard covers40 CFR 503 and the NOP tell you when a pile has been hot enough for long enough to reduce pathogens. That is a well-defined question with a published answer, and the bench holds you to it.

What it does not coverWhether the compost is mature — stable, finished, safe to pot into. Time-and-temperature is a pathogen criterion, not a maturity criterion, and a pile can satisfy the first while failing the second badly enough to stunt everything you plant in it.

What this bench does about itSays so, rather than letting a green temperature chart imply an answer to a question it never asked. Maturity is assessed by bioassay, respirometry or a seedling test, none of which a phone can do — so the bench points at the gap instead of papering over it.

The same three-way gate governs every page here: ship it with a source, ship it labelled a convention, or refuse and take your value instead. ADR-031 has the reasoning.
