The run — cross, plants, traits

Name the cross, add your plants, and set the traits you'll score with a weight for how much each matters to your program. Works for any crop — apples, tomatoes, hops, hemp or cannabis where legal. Your run saves in this browser; export before you clear it.

"Set plants" renumbers to #1…#N and keeps any scores already entered for existing numbers. Individual plants can be culled later without renumbering.

Traits & weights

Weight 2 counts double in the total. Typical fruit program: flavor ×2, texture, yield, disease resistance ×1.5, storage. Typical flower program: aroma ×2, structure, vigor, resin, mold resistance ×1.5, ripening speed.

Score — plant by plant

Tap a plant, then rate each trait 1–5. Come back each check-in and adjust — the board always shows the current picture. Notes hold the phenotype description ("short internodes, grape candy nose").

Selection board

Ranked by weighted score. Tap ⭐ to keep, ✕ to cull. The readout below is the real quantitative-genetics number: how far your keepers sit above the run.

Mothers & planned crosses

A keeper you hold in veg and cut clones from is a mother — tap 🌿 on the board to promote one. Plan next season's crosses here; both ride along in the export.

Honest note: a clone is a genetic copy — cloning a mother preserves her pheno but doesn't advance the line. Only the seed run from a cross does, and R = h² × S applies to that seed run, not to the copies.

Segregation check

Counting a yes/no trait across the run — purple stems, resistance, early ripening? Enter the two counts and test the classic ratios: a fit to 3:1 says single dominant gene, 1:1 says a testcross-style split, 9:7 hints at two complementary genes.

χ² with 1 degree of freedom; the p < 0.05 cutoff is 3.84. Small runs are weak evidence either way — a clean call usually needs 30+ plants. The .eco cross: line runs the same test in a graded protocol.
